Meow Widget — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-10-29

Meow Widget ("the app") is an Android virtual pet made by iMango Studio ("we"): a cat you feed, pet and play with, which can also float above your other apps. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1. Summary

Your cat lives entirely on your phone. We (iMango Studio) don't collect any personal data: there are no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours that receive data from the app. If the app crashes, it sends a technical crash report through Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the bug (section 5); you can turn this off in Settings. The app also includes Google's AdMob SDK so that the Settings screen may show a banner ad; when it does, the AdMob SDK collects some device data to show ads, as described in section 4. The main screen and the floating cat never show ads. Your cat, its name, its history and your settings stay on your phone and are never shared with AdMob or anyone else. We don't sell any data.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your cat's name, its mood, energy and fullness, its friendship level, when you last fed, petted and played with it, a count of these per day and a journal of care and outings for the last 30 days, and your settings (floating mode, size, position, transparency, sound, quiet hours, reminders, language). They are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

If you use Backup & restore in Settings, the app writes your cat (the data above, without your settings) to a file in a place you choose with Android's file picker, and reads it back only when you pick it to restore. The file stays where you put it; we never receive it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Settings screen may show a banner ad from Google AdMob, a third-party service run by Google. Ads never appear on the main screen, on the floating cat or above other apps. To serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and basic device and app information
  • Diagnostics, such as ad performance and crash data from the SDK

This data goes directly to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can limit personalized ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone). If your phone is offline or no ad is available, no banner is shown.

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics, a service run by Google for us, sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • The technical details of the crash (which code failed, the app version)
  • Your phone's model, Android version, available memory and storage, and whether it is rooted
  • A random identifier for this installation of the app, not linked to you or to your advertising ID

Reports never include your cat, its name, its history or your settings. They are kept by Firebase for 90 days and used only to fix bugs; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. To stop sending them, turn off Settings → Send crash reports.

6. Children

Meow Widget does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects nothing about the person using it; crash reports (section 5) are technical only, and ads, where shown, come from Google AdMob as described in section 4. There are no purchases, no chat and no way to share data with other people.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
