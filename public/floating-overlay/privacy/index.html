Floating Overlay — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-10-13

Floating Overlay ("the app") is an Android utility made by iMango Studio ("we"). It shows a small floating panel above other apps with the date and time, the name of the app you are using and a label you write, plus a screenshot button. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1. Summary

The app does not collect any personal or device data. It has no internet permission, so nothing it handles can leave your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that send data. We don't sell or share any data.

2. What the app accesses on your device

AccessWhyWhat happens to it
Display over other appsDraw the floating panel on top of other appsShown on screen only
Usage accessRead which app is currently in the foreground, to show its name on the panelOnly the latest app is read; it is kept in memory and never saved or sent
Screen capture (Android's screen-sharing dialog)Take a screenshot when you tap the camera button, after you approve the system dialogSaved as a PNG file in Pictures/Screenshots/FloatingOverlay on your device only
NotificationsShow the notification Android requires while the panel or a capture session is running, with Hide / Settings / Turn off / Stop buttonsLocal only
Storage (Android 8–9 only)Save screenshots to the Pictures folderLocal only

You can turn any of these off in Android settings at any time; the app keeps working with what is left (for example, without usage access the panel shows no app name).

3. Data stored on your device

Your settings (panel size, position, opacity, date format, language, capture mode) and your custom label are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Screenshots stay in your Pictures folder until you delete them.

4. Children

Floating Overlay is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
