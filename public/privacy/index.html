iMango Studio — Privacy Policy

Applies to all iMango Studio apps and games · Effective date: 2026-10-19

This policy covers every mobile app and game published by iMango Studio ("we"), including test builds distributed through Google Play closed or internal testing. Where an app has its own privacy policy page on this site, that page applies to that app instead.

1. Summary

Our apps do not collect, store or share any personal data. We have no servers that receive information from the apps, there are no accounts or sign-ins, and we don't sell data to anyone.

2. Data we collect

None. The apps do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and they do not send usage data to us.

3. Data on your device

Some apps keep basic settings or game progress (for example, sound on/off or the last level you reached) in the app's private storage on your phone, so they work the way you left them. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it.

4. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. Apps are downloaded and updated through Google Play, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy. If you send us feedback by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

5. Children

Our apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If an app starts handling data differently (for example, by adding analytics or ads), it will get its own privacy policy page describing exactly what is collected, and this page will be updated. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.
