Lẩu Nhà Mình · Hotpot Party

A virtual hotpot with friends: make a table, share the code, cook, grab and eat together.

How to play

  • 2–4 players share one pot. Drag ingredients into the broth, wait until they are cooked, then grab them before anyone else.
  • Cooked food scores, raw food costs points and overcooked food is worth nothing. Don't overload the pot!
  • Finish your own secret menu before your stomach is full for a big bonus.
  • Create a table and send the code or an invite link, quick-match with other players, or play alone with bots.
  • Emotes, quick chat, friends, and end-of-round titles like "Vua gắp".
  • Play in the browser or on Android, together on the same server.
