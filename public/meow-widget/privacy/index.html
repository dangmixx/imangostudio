Meow Widget — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-10-27

Meow Widget ("the app") is an Android virtual pet made by iMango Studio ("we"): a cat you feed, pet and play with, which can also float above your other apps. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1. Summary

The app works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We don't collect, share or sell any data, and we have no server that could receive it.

2. What the app accesses on your device

AccessWhyWhat happens to it
Display over other appsShow your cat on top of other apps, only after you turn floating mode onShown on screen only
Foreground serviceKeep the floating cat on screen while you use other appsRuns on your phone only; stops when you turn floating mode off
NotificationsShow the notification Android requires while the cat floats, with Hide / Show / Turn off buttonsLocal only; the app sends no other notifications
Vibration (haptic feedback)A short tick when you pick the floating cat up or drag it over the ✕Local only

You can turn off display over other apps and notifications in Android settings at any time. Without them your cat simply stays inside the app.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your cat's name, its mood and energy, when you last fed, petted and played with it, a count of these per day for the last 30 days, and your settings (floating mode, size, position, sound, language). They are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

4. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links that share data.

5. Changes

If the app ever adds something that handles data differently (for example ads), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
