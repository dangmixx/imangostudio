Fish Dash

Hold to swim up, let go to sink, and slip through the gaps between the rocks.

How to play

  • Hold the screen to swim up, release to sink. Squeeze through the gaps between rocks.
  • Swim through ocean zones, each with its own hazards: fish schools, kelp, sand jets, electric jellyfish.
  • Face a boss at the end of every zone, from the octopus to the whale.
  • Collect coins and shields, then unlock fish, bubble and shield skins in the shop.
  • Plays in English and Vietnamese.
