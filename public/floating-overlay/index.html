Floating Overlay

A small panel that floats above every app: the time, the app you are in, your own label and a screenshot button.

Features

  • Date and time to the second, in the format you choose.
  • The name of the app in the foreground, so you always know where you are (handy for testing, demos and screen recordings).
  • A custom label such as WORK, TEST ACCOUNT or DEVICE 01.
  • One-tap screenshots, saved to Pictures/Screenshots/FloatingOverlay. The panel hides itself for the shot unless you want it in.
  • Drag it anywhere, hide it to a small bubble, pick the size and opacity; light and dark mode.
  • English, Vietnamese, French, Chinese and Japanese.
  • No internet access, no ads, no data collected.
