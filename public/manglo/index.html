Manglo

Beautiful filters. Natural you. A simple photo editor that keeps skin tones natural, with every edit done on your phone.

Features

  • Filters such as Natural, Soft, Warm, Film and Fresh, tuned so skin doesn't turn orange, red or grey, each with a strength slider.
  • Save your own looks (filter, strength and adjustments) and apply them to any photo in one tap.
  • Brightness, contrast and saturation; crop freely or to 1:1, 4:5, 9:16; rotate and flip; hold to compare with the original; undo and redo.
  • Saves at full resolution, even 48 MP photos, as JPEG or lossless PNG. Location data is removed from saved photos by default.
  • Free, no account. The only ad is an optional short video to try a Premium filter, and your photos never leave your phone.
