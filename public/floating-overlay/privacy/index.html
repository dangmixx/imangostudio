Floating Overlay — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-10-20

Floating Overlay ("the app") is an Android utility made by iMango Studio ("we"). It shows a small floating panel above other apps with the date and time, the name of the app you are using and a label you write, plus a screenshot button. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1. Summary

We (iMango Studio) don't collect any personal data: there are no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours that receive data from the app. The settings screen shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, and the AdMob SDK collects some device data to show ads, as described in section 4. The floating panel itself never shows ads. What the panel shows (the app you are using, your label, your screenshots) stays on your phone and is never shared with AdMob or anyone else. We don't sell any data.

You can turn off display over other apps, usage access, screen capture and notifications in Android settings at any time; the app keeps working with what is left (for example, without usage access the panel shows no app name).

3. Data stored on your device

Your settings (panel size, position, opacity, date format, language, capture mode) and your custom label are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Screenshots stay in your Pictures folder until you delete them.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The settings screen shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, a third-party service run by Google. To serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and basic device and app information
  • Diagnostics, such as ad performance and crash data from the SDK

This data goes directly to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you. If your phone is offline or no ad is available, no banner is shown.

5. Children

Floating Overlay is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
