Meow Paws

Tap cute cat tiles, match three of a kind and clear the board.

How to play

  • Tap tiles that are not covered by others to send them to your tray.
  • Three matching tiles in the tray clear away. The tray holds seven tiles, so plan ahead.
  • Clear every tile to finish the level. Match quickly for combos.
  • Seasonal events with special levels, outfits for the cat mascot and an Advent calendar.
  • Plays fully offline, no ads, no sign-in.
