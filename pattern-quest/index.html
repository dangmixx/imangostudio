Meow Paws: Pattern Quest

Puzzles that make software design patterns click. No code, just thinking.

How it works

  • Every level is a small everyday story: spot what's going wrong, pick the best fix, and only then does the pattern reveal its name.
  • Four kinds of puzzles: Identify, Match, Fix, and Build, where you drag blocks into place to assemble the solution.
  • 10 classic patterns in three worlds (Structure, Behavior, Creation), plus comparison levels like Strategy vs State.
  • 52 handcrafted levels, a Pattern Library, a Daily Challenge, achievements, and Mango the studious cat cheering you on.
  • Works offline, in English and Vietnamese.
