Baby Diary — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-11-11

Baby Diary ("the app") is an Android baby tracker made by iMango Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone and what little data it sends.

1. Summary

Everything you record about your baby — profile, feeding, sleep, diapers, growth, milestones, health notes and any photos you add — is stored on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours, so this information is never uploaded to us. The app is free and supported by ads. It uses three Google services: Google AdMob for ads (section 4), Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous usage statistics (section 5), and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports (section 6). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first (section 7). We don't sell any data.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves what you enter in its private storage on your phone: your baby's name, date of birth and photo; feeding, sleep and diaper logs; weight, height and growth records; milestones and health or vaccination notes; reminders; and any photos you add to the diary. This data stays on your device, we cannot see it, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it. Photos you choose are read only to show them in the diary and are not uploaded.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party service run by Google. To serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and basic device and app information

This data goes directly to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Nothing you record about your baby is sent to AdMob. If your phone is offline, no ad is shown.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which features are used and make the app better, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • App events: which screens are opened and which actions are used (for example, that a feeding, sleep or diaper entry was saved) — the type of action only, never the values or notes you entered
  • Information Firebase collects on its own: an app instance ID, your device's advertising ID when your ad consent allows it, device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate location (country / city) derived from your IP address, and when and how long the app is opened

None of your baby's details are part of these statistics. We see them only as totals in Google Analytics; they are not linked to your name or to any account. Google keeps them for up to 14 months. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report so we can fix the problem. A report includes the type of crash and where it happened in the code, your device model and operating system, the app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. It does not include your baby's data or the content you entered. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad or statistic. Without consent to store information on your device, no statistics are collected and ads are not personalized. You can change your answer at any time in the app's Settings → Ad and data privacy choices.
  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone) limits ads and statistics.
  • Because your baby's data lives only on your phone, you can remove all of it by deleting entries in the app or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • To ask us a question about data, email us at the address below. Statistics and crash reports are not linked to a person, and Google deletes them after its retention period.

8. Children

Baby Diary is made for parents and caregivers — adults — to record information about their own child. It is not directed at children under 13 as users, and we do not knowingly let children create entries or collect data from them. The information you record about your child is stored on your device and is never uploaded to us.

9. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if we add or change a feature, SDK or ad format), we update this page and the effective date above.
