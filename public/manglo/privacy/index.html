Manglo — Privacy Policy

iMango Studio · Effective date: 2026-10-20

Manglo ("the app") is an Android photo editor made by iMango Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your photos.

1. Summary

Your photos stay on your phone. Every edit is processed on your device, and the app has no internet permission, so nothing it handles can leave your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We don't collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

2. What the app accesses on your device

AccessWhyWhat happens to it
Photos you pick (Android photo picker)Open the photo you want to edit. The app only gets the photos you choose, never your whole libraryEdited on your device only; never uploaded

3. Data stored on your device

Your saved looks, favorites and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Temporary files made while saving a photo are deleted right after. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this. Photos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs that send data. It is downloaded and updated through Google Play, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy.

5. Children

Manglo does not collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a paid upgrade is added later), we update this page and the effective date above.
