Fish Dash

Privacy Policy
Effective: 3 October 2026 · iMango Studio

Summary

Fish Dash has no accounts, and we do not collect or store any personal information on our own servers. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to serve those ads, as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no purchases with real money. We do not receive your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small save file on your own device only. It contains:

  • your best score and the furthest zone you reached;
  • in-game coins, and the fish, bubble and shield skins you own and use;
  • your settings (music and sound volume, language) and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This file never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad on the start and game-over screens, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to revive or to earn coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped.

This data goes directly to Google; we do not receive it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads. The game itself plays without a connection.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback during play.

Third-party services

Google Play (download and updates) and Google AdMob (ads), both under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Fish Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is used (for example adding sign-in, analytics or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
